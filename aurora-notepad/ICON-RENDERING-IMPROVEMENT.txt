Aurora Notepad Title Icon Rendering
===================================

Source asset
------------

The supplied `aurora-notepad.png` is 814x820 RGBA artwork. It contains fine
notebook rings, page lines, a gray outline, and a blue-gray side/bottom shadow.
At the 16 logical-pixel titlebar size, those details are close to the sampling
limit.

Diagnosis
---------

The original direct 814x820 -> 16px reduction produced hard gray/blue blocks,
especially around the top rings and right edge. The PNG also contains fully
transparent pixels with black RGB values. Straight-alpha linear sampling can
expose those RGB values during reduction.

Improvement
-----------

The first improvement used `aurora-notepad-title.png` as a 64x64
Lanczos-prefiltered derivative generated from the supplied source. That fixed
the hard reduction blocks, but the source's outline/shadow still collapsed
into a slight left strip at 16 px.

The title derivative is now a purpose-built 64x64 flat variant that preserves
the same notebook identity: clean page, blue header, four rings, and page
rules, with no external side/bottom shadow. The original `aurora-notepad.png`
and native `aurora-notepad.ico` remain unchanged. Transparent edge RGB is
sanitized before Aurora renders the derivative.

Remaining left detail
---------------------

The left-side artifact was removed from the titlebar variant. The original
large artwork still retains its own outline/shadow for the native icon.

Verification
------------

- `build/icon-left-inspect.png`: direct visual inspection before the derivative.
- `build/icon-prefiltered-crop.png`: intermediate prefiltered variant.
- `build/icon-flat-crop.png`: final clean titlebar variant.
- `tests/headless_smoke.d`: passes after the icon change.
